# 01. Target Users / Personas

Status: agreed

## Primary launch persona

**Individual self-directed learner** who wants to keep their mind sharp in key topics by using spare time on their phone and studying from their own resources.

## Core profile

- **User type:** Solo learner
- **Primary device/context:** Phone, in spare moments, with a daily usage rhythm
- **Primary content source:** Self-authored material
- **Primary content types:** Concept cards and quiz cards
- **Primary topic category:** Professional knowledge
- **Secondary topic category:** Language learning

## Core learning job

- **Primary job:** Memorize terms and link them to concepts
- **Secondary job:** Understand concepts through practical questions

## Product expectations implied by persona

- **Offline-first is critical** for core usage
- **Single-user at launch** is acceptable
- Product should still be **designed for future sharing/export portability**
- The product should sit **between notes apps and flashcard apps**
- Concept cards should support **title + markdown body + small images**
- The experience should support a **daily review habit** rather than requiring long study sessions

## Biggest pain point with current alternatives

Existing tools are either note-taking apps or flashcard apps, but the target user wants something in between: a flash-card-like system that supports richer structured content and small images.

## 30-day success outcome

Within 30 days, the user has created a small personal knowledge base for one important topic, reviewed it daily, and feels noticeably faster and more confident recalling terms and explaining core concepts.

## Design implications for the MVP

1. **Authoring must be fast** on mobile.
2. **Review must be lightweight** and easy to resume daily.
3. **Content structure must be richer than simple front/back flashcards.**
4. **Offline access cannot be optional** for core browsing, editing, and study flows.
5. **Organization matters** because professional knowledge benefits from topic hierarchy.
