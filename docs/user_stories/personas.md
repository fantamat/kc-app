# Personas Working Draft

## Primary launch persona

- **Persona:** Individual self-directed learner
- **Goal:** Keep their mind sharp in key topics
- **Usage pattern:** Uses spare time on their phone
- **Content source:** Their own resources
- **Content format:** Concept cards and quiz cards

## Topic categories for MVP

- **Primary topic category:** Professional knowledge
- **Secondary topic category:** Language learning

## Core learning job

- **Primary:** Memorizing terms and linking them to concepts
- **Secondary:** Understanding concepts via practical questions

## Product expectations

- **Offline priority:** Critical
- **Launch collaboration model:** Single-user at launch, but design for future sharing
- **Biggest current pain point:** Existing tools are either notes apps or flashcards, but the user wants something in between: a flash-card-like system that supports structured content and small images.
- **Preferred concept-card format:** Title + markdown body + images
- **Expected usage frequency:** Once per day

## Draft interpretation

This suggests the MVP is optimized for a solo mobile learner who:
- curates their own study material instead of consuming a shared catalog,
- values quick, opportunistic review sessions,
- wants lightweight organization of topics,
- primarily studies professional knowledge,
- secondarily studies language learning material,
- needs strong support for memorizing terms and connecting them to concepts,
- benefits from practical question-based reinforcement,
- expects core usage to work without connectivity,
- should not be blocked on account/login for everyday use,
- wants a product positioned between freeform notes and simplistic flashcards,
- prefers markdown as the core formatting model for concept cards,
- values richer card content including small supporting images,
- and is likely to engage in a daily review habit rather than constant micro-sessions.
